 Data Types
 . String - {% assign my_string = "String Data Type" %} - length of string {{ my_string.size }}
 . Number - {% assign my_number = 31 %} - {% assign my_floating_number = 31.31 %}
 . Boolean - {% assign my_boolean = true %} - output will be true not 1
 . Nil - {% assign my_nil = nil %}

 Filters:

 Math filters:

 . plus - addition
 . minus - subtraction
 . times - multiply
 . divided_by - divide
 . modulo - mod
 . round - round fraction
 . floor - round to floor
 . ceil - round to ceil
 . abs - absolute value (positive)
 . at_most - provide at_most value if actual is greater than at_most
 . at_lease - provide at_least value if actual is smaller than at_least

 String filters:

 . upcase
 . downcase
 . capitalize
 . append - append string
 . strip - removes extra whitespaces
 . strip_html - returns text inside html tags
 . truncate - add ... at end of specified length of characters
 . truncatewords - add ... at end of specified length of words
 . newline_to_br - add line breaks in paragraph
 . replace - replace text with specified text in string
 . replace_first
 . replace_last
 . remove - remove specified text from string
 . remove_first
 . remove_last
